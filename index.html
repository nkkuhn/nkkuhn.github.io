Welcome to the Kühn lab

Dendrites, Circuits and Behavior.

Our lab investigates how the brain transforms sensory information into rapid and appropriate actions. We focus on the superior colliculus, a sensory-motor hub that converts visual signals into behaviors such as orienting, freezing, avoidance, and escape. Our central goal is to understand how dendrites shape circuit activity and how these circuit computations give rise to flexible behavior.

The lab is especially interested in dendrites. Dendrites are the tree-like structures through which neurons receive thousands of inputs. Rather than acting as passive cables, dendrites can filter, amplify, suppress, and combine incoming signals through their morphology and ion channels. We study how these dendritic computations influence the activity of defined neural circuits and help animals identify which signals are relevant.
